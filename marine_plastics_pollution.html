Three Connected Crises, One Environmental Problem

Plastic pollution doesn't just destroy marine life; it weakens the blue and white systems responsible for climate regulation and carbon sequestration.

SDG 14 Target

Marine Life Protection

  • Microplastic ingestion disrupts liver and gut function in fish.
  • Ghost nets cause millions of marine mammal deaths.
  • Disruption of the food chain leading to humans.

Plastic Ingestion & Toxicity

Plastic particles absorb heavy toxic pollutants and Persistent Organic Pollutants (POPs) from seawater. When ingested by marine life, these toxins transfer to tissues, causing hormonal disorders, liver damage, and reduced reproductive capacity.

Scientific Reference: Alimba & Faggio (2019) - Ecotoxicological impacts on marine organisms.
